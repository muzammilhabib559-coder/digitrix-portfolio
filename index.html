How we plan

The Framework

The same four questions guide every paid media plan we build.

Strategy & channel mix

Where the budget goes

We map the customer journey — awareness, consideration, decision — then split spend across channels based on where your customers actually are, not a fixed formula.

Channels we weigh

  • Meta Ads — broad-reach awareness & retargeting on Facebook/Instagram
  • Google Ads — capturing existing intent on Search & Shopping
  • TikTok Ads — short-form creative for younger, discovery-led audiences
  • Email & SMS — low-cost retention once a channel proves itself
Social campaign architecture

Built in funnel stages

Every social campaign runs in layers: top-of-funnel awareness, mid-funnel retargeting & lookalikes, and bottom-of-funnel conversion — each with its own creative and objective.

Audiences we build

  • Interest & behaviour audiences — for cold, top-of-funnel reach
  • Lookalike audiences — modelled on existing buyers or leads
  • Retargeting pools — site visitors, video viewers & cart abandoners
  • At least two creative concepts tested per stage before scaling spend
Search intent & keywords

Matched to buyer intent

On Google, keywords get grouped by intent — research, comparison, ready-to-buy — with negative keyword lists built in early to protect spend.

Intent layers we target

  • Research intent — broad/phrase match, top-of-funnel education terms
  • Comparison intent — "best," "vs" & alternative-brand terms
  • Ready-to-buy intent — exact match on high-purchase-intent phrases
  • Negative keyword lists reviewed weekly to cut wasted spend
Data-driven optimization

Diagnose before you spend more

When clicks look good but conversions don't follow, we check landing pages, audience-message match and tracking before ever touching the budget.

Diagnostic checks we run

  • Landing page experience — load speed, mobile layout, message match
  • Audience-message match — is the ad promise the one the page delivers?
  • Tracking & attribution — pixel/conversion setup checked before blaming targeting
  • Budget reallocated only after these three are confirmed clean
